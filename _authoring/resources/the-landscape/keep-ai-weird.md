# Keep AI Weird

## Overview

This article explores the danger of "sanitizing" AI's inherent weirdness and creative potential through excessive content filtering and optimization for business values. Drawing parallels between San Francisco's counterculture history and AI's evolution, the author argues that AI's "hallucinations" and unexpected outputs represent valuable digital creativity that should be preserved, not eliminated.

---

## The Core Question: AI's Inherent Strangeness

### Kyle Turman's Spark

**Key Observation:** Kyle Turman (creative technologist, staff designer at Anthropic) observed: "AI is actually really weird, and I don't think people appreciate that enough."

**Author's Question:** Are we at risk of sanitizing AI's inherent strangeness?

### The Sanitization Danger

**The Slippery Slope:** Foundation AI models risk being "cleansed" of all undesirable elements—a revisionist optimization process that could suppress valuable aspects of human-like behavior.

**Current Reality:** LLMs are reflections of humanity with all our "weird and idiosyncratic quirks that make us human"—albeit currently reflecting American and Western perspectives.

**Risk:** Training foundation models to maximize business values of American capitalism could suppress "radical and non-conforming ideas"—exactly the kinds of thinking that drive innovation.

**The Pattern:** Maximize business value → Suppress unconventional thinking → Eliminate edge cases and unexpected outputs → Standardization and homogenization.

## AI's Own History of Weirdness

### Early AI Experiments: The Edge States

**AARON (1972)**
- Created by Harold Cohen
- Trained basic model on artistic decision-making
- Output: Outsider art-like compositions
- Significance: Early exploration of computational creativity

**Racter (1984)**
- Early text-generating AI
- Output: Dreamlike and surrealist language patterns
- Notable quote: "More than iron, more than lead, more than gold I need electricity. I need it more than I need lamb or pork or lettuce or cucumber. I need it for my dreams."
- Significance: Unexpected poetic and emotional resonance despite simple architecture

**Google Deep Dream (2015)**
- Convolutional neural network
- Mechanism: Looks for patterns found in training data
- Output: Hallucination-like images and videos; psychedelic visualizations
- Significance: Demonstrated how pattern-seeking can generate aesthetic experiences

### The Importance of Edge States

**Definition:** "Edge states" = moments where AI produces unexpected, creative, or surreal outputs that diverge from deterministic prediction.

**Author's Assessment:** "These 'edge states' in AI's evolution are, to me, the most interesting, and human, expressions."

**Analogy:** Similar to "liminal space" in human creativity—the threshold between reality and imagination where creative insights emerge.

## Liminal Space: The Threshold of Creativity

### Defining Liminal Space

**Concept:** Liminal space is "the threshold between reality and imagination" where meaning extraction and creative synthesis occur.

**Human Process:** Mental process of extracting meaning from liminal space involves:
1. Looking for patterns
2. Synthesizing new ideas and information
3. Finding unexpected connections
4. Contextualizing findings
5. Articulating ideas into expressible words

**AI Parallel (Transformer Architecture):**
1. **Attention Mechanism:** Looks for patterns in training data
2. **Neural Networks:** "Synthesize" information from pattern recognition
3. **Iteration & Prioritization:** Form probabilistic insights from pattern synthesis
4. **Positional Encoding:** Maps information to broader context
5. **Token Generation:** Articulates output as best guess based on prior knowledge

**Structural Analogy:** The transformer architecture mirrors human creative cognition at a fundamental level—both operate in liminal space between known patterns and novel combinations.

### Why This Matters

The parallel between human and AI creativity suggests that AI's "weird" outputs aren't failures—they're expressions of a computational process analogous to human creativity.

## Reframing AI Hallucinations

### The Current Problem Perspective

**Standard Narrative:** AI "hallucinations" = dangerous, unreliable, problematic outputs to be eliminated.

**Real-World Impact:** When AI generates plausible but false information, it creates genuine harm and unreliability.

### A Nuanced Alternative View

**Author's Perspective:** "It's not making things up, it's just synthesizing a highly probable answer from an ambiguous cloud of understanding (question, data, meaning, etc.)."

**Distinction:** 
- **Problem framing:** "Making things up" = intentional deception
- **Accurate description:** Synthesizing probable answers from ambiguous understanding = mathematical process, not deception

**The Proposal:** "Let's explore and celebrate this analog of human creativity. What if, instead of fearing AI's 'hallucinations,' we embraced them as digital dreams?"

### Two Valid Perspectives

**Responsible AI Perspective (Valid):** Unreliable outputs create harm in high-stakes domains (healthcare, law, finance) and should be minimized.

**Creative Exploration Perspective (Also Valid):** Same "hallucination" mechanism generates novel insights, unexpected connections, and creative output worth preserving and exploring.

**The Tension:** Both are true simultaneously—same capability creates both problems and possibilities.

**Referenced Articles:**

### The Tension Between Ethics and Creativity

**Ethical Concerns:**
- Training data sourced from creators without consent
- Displacement of human creative labor
- Consolidation of power in large tech companies
- Erosion of cultural commons

**Creative Possibilities:**
- New forms of human-AI creative collaboration
- Democratized access to creative tools
- Exploration of novel aesthetic spaces

**The Challenge:** These perspectives aren't reconcilable into a single "correct" stance. Both are legitimate and in genuine tension.
