# IxDA // Interaction 19

My talk on data sovereignty and design tactics for AI from IxDA's annual conference.

## Overview

At the annual IxDA Interaction 19 conference, I presented alongside design luminaries like John Maeda and Bill Buxton. My talk focused on data sovereignty and design tactics for AI, emphasizing the crucial role of interaction designers as gatekeepers of customer data. I showcased the Serendipity Watch project, illustrating how playfulness and user empowerment can create deeply intimate experiences that learn from user interactions.

## Key Themes

### Designers as Data Gatekeepers
- Designers are uniquely positioned to influence how personal data is collected and protected
- Ethical responsibility to safeguard user information and maintain trust
- Trust is foundational for user engagement with AI technologies

### Playfulness as Design Tactic
- Playfulness encourages user interaction and data generation
- Playful designs lead to more robust AI models through natural engagement
- Reduces barriers to entry, makes technology more approachable

### Serendipity Watch Demo
- Watch empowers users by controlling their interactions and data
- Demonstrates personalization without compromising privacy
- Device learns preferences through playful interactions

## Notable Moment

Shared the stage with design heroes John Maeda and Bill Buxton, highlighting the collaborative spirit of the design community.
