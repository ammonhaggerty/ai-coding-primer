## Key Points

- **Audience:** Non-coders, designers, PMs, founders, researchers, hobbyists. Also lapsed engineers and creative technologists.

- **Tone:** Practical, grounded, zero hype. "Friend who's done it." Bungee jump analogy — scarier to look at than to do.
- **Central argument:** The most important skills are imagination and curiosity.
- **Automation philosophy:** Every step that can be automated should be. First terminal interaction = typing `claude`.
